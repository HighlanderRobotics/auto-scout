Field landmark labeler

Label points fixed to the floor or field boundary. Do not click robots, hub tops, or a point hidden by a robot. The first four rows can be changed to match landmarks from the game manual. Each landmark needs its known top-down field position: x=0 is blue end, x=100 is red end; y=0 is far side, y=100 is near side.
