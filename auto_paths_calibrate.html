Calibrate the fixed field camera

Click four floor landmarks in clockwise order on the reference image. Enter their corresponding top-down positions as percentages: x=0 at the blue end, x=100 at the red end; y=0 at the far side and y=100 at the near side. Use coplanar floor points, not hub tops or robot centers. If all four field corners are visible, their default coordinates below can be used. Otherwise enter known positions of the chosen landmarks.

Then set the field-camera crop to exclude scoreboards and inset cameras. This tool does not automatically detect the match clock or camera changes.
